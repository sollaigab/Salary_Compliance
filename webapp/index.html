How many job ads say what they pay

Since 7 June 2026, job ads in Italy must state the starting pay or a pay range. This page measures how many actually do, and how much they offer.

Methodology and limits

Sources

The ads come from company career sites, read through the public APIs and feeds of their applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Oracle Recruiting, SuccessFactors). No logins and no third-party job boards are used. Locations are matched to official lists of municipalities: ISTAT for Italy, Eurostat (LAU 2023 and NUTS 2021) for the other countries.

Collection rules

The collection respects robots.txt and text and data mining opt-outs (art. 70-quater of Italian Law 633/1941). Requests are spaced out and stop when a service asks for a pause. The ad text is not republished, and recruiters' personal data is not stored.

Protecting the companies

Only aggregate data is published. A combination appears if it covers at least ads from at least different companies and no company has more than of the ads. When only one combination in a group is hidden, the smallest of the others is hidden too, so it cannot be worked out by subtraction. For the same reason, a filtered figure is hidden when removing the filter would leave a small group, such as the few ads of one company.

What is measured

An ad "states the pay" if it gives a figure or a range, in the text or in the dedicated field of the applicant tracking system. Monthly figures are converted to gross annual salary over 14 monthly payments in Italy, the Italian norm, and over 12 in Germany, France and the Netherlands (where the 8% holiday allowance, paid separately, is not added); internships always over 12. The main analysis covers ads published from 7 June 2026, the deadline for every EU country to transpose the pay transparency directive (2023/970).

Countries

Italy is the main subject: its obligation applies from 7 June 2026. Germany, the Netherlands and France are observed as a comparison, with the same method: according to the published legislative timetables they had not transposed the directive by that date. Ads are counted in the country where the job is located; the few ads for remote work anywhere in Europe, with no country, are left out.

Work arrangement

Taken from the applicant tracking system when it has a field for it, otherwise from the ad text. When neither says, roles that can only be done in person (store, kitchen, plant, network and clinic staff) are counted as on site.

Limits

  • Only ads online since are observed: older ads still online are not a sample of the market before the law.
  • The data covers of the companies analyzed. Companies whose systems have no public source are left out, so the results describe these companies, not the whole market.
  • Pay, seniority and work arrangement are extracted from the text with automatic rules, which are being checked by hand on samples of ads.
  • The publication date means slightly different things across applicant tracking systems.
