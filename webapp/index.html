How many job ads say what they pay

Since 7 June 2026, job ads in Italy must state the starting pay or a pay range. This page measures how many actually do, and how much they offer.

Methodology and limits

Sources

The ads come from company career sites, read through the public APIs and feeds of their applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, Workable, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Oracle Recruiting, SuccessFactors). No logins and no third-party job boards are used.

Collection rules

The collection respects robots.txt and text and data mining opt-outs (art. 70-quater of Italian Law 633/1941). Requests are spaced out and stop when a service asks for a pause. The ad text is not republished, and recruiters' personal data is not stored.

Protecting the companies

Only data aggregated by sector is published. A combination appears if it covers at least ads from at least different companies and no company has more than of the ads. When only one combination in a group is hidden, the smallest of the others is hidden too, so it cannot be worked out by subtraction.

What is measured

An ad "states the pay" if it gives a figure or a range, in the text or in the dedicated field of the applicant tracking system. Monthly figures are converted to gross annual salary over 14 monthly payments, the Italian norm (12 for internships). The main analysis covers ads published from 7 June 2026.

Limits

  • Only ads online since are observed: older ads still online are not a sample of the market before the law.
  • The data covers of the companies analyzed. Companies whose systems have no public source are left out, so the results describe these companies, not the whole market.
  • Pay and seniority are extracted from the text with automatic rules, which are being checked by hand on a sample of ads.
  • The publication date means slightly different things across applicant tracking systems.
